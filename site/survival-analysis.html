Title

Survival analysis — Kaplan–Meier and Cox

README lines 1–1

Question

How does observed survival differ by recorded sex, and how are age, sex and physician-rated ECOG performance associated with mortality after mutual adjustment?

README lines 9–10

Results

Median KM survival was 270 days for male patients and 426 days for female patients. The unadjusted log-rank statistic was 10.008174 (1 degree of freedom), p=0.00155847.

README lines 59–61

Limitations

Historical observational advanced lung cancer cohort; generalization and unmeasured confounding limit interpretation. Sex is the dataset's recorded binary variable; no treatment effect is estimated.
