Title

RNA-seq differential expression — reproducing a published study

README lines 1–1

Question

Can the differentially expressed genes reported for GSE52778 (dexamethasone vs untreated) be reproduced with an independent pipeline, and how close are the gene sets and gene rankings?

README lines 9–10

Results

Recovery. 301 of the 302 published significant genes in the common universe are also significant in pyDESeq2 (99.7%), all 301 in the same direction. The only miss is RASD1 (published q = 0.016). 14 published significant genes are not tested by pyDESeq2 (symbol mismatch or filtered).

README lines 51–53

Limitations

The original Cuffdiff run (version, annotation, exact sample grouping, pooling of replicates) is not documented in the deposited table; differences are reported as method differences. Whether the table pools only dexamethasone-alone samples or also the albuterol groups is not stated and was not determined.
