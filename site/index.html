P11README evidence

RNA-seq differential expression — reproducing a published study

Can the differentially expressed genes reported for GSE52778 (dexamethasone vs untreated) be reproduced with an independent pipeline, and how close are the gene sets and gene rankings?

Reported result

Recovery. 301 of the 302 published significant genes in the common universe are also significant in pyDESeq2 (99.7%), all 301 in the same direction. The only miss is RASD1 (published q = 0.016). 14 published significant genes are not tested by pyDESeq2 (symbol mismatch or filtered).

Scope & limitation

The original Cuffdiff run (version, annotation, exact sample grouping, pooling of replicates) is not documented in the deposited table; differences are reported as method differences. Whether the table pools only dexamethasone-alone samples or also the albuterol groups is not stated and was not determined.

P13README evidence

How well does pLDDT predict structural accuracy?

pLDDT is the per-residue confidence reported by AlphaFold and ESMFold. Does it track the actual accuracy of a model against an experimental structure, i.e. is it calibrated (a residue with pLDDT 80 is ~0.80 correct)? Does that hold for structures the predictors could not have seen, and for regions that are disordered?

Reported result

Both predictors are close to the diagonal: ECE = 0.016 (95 % CI 0.013–0.022; 36,467 residues) for ESMFold and 0.012 (0.004–0.024; 30,196 residues) for AlphaFold DB.

Scope & limitation

Accuracy reference is a single crystal structure. lDDT against one X-ray model penalises genuine flexibility and crystal-packing differences; lDDT is computed on Cα atoms only (not all-atom lDDT), residues are scored equally rather than contact-weighted, and chains were evaluated alone (no ligand, partner or oligomer context).

P14README evidence

Dose–response toolkit — how reliable is an IC50?

How reliable are IC50 estimates from 4PL fits — in particular, how often is a reported interval right, and when should the number not be reported at all?

Reported result

With the IC50 well inside the range, the median error is ≈0 and RMSE ≈ 0.09 log10 (≈ 23%); the nominal 95% interval covered the truth in about 90–91% of the datasets that were reported — slightly anti-conservative.

Scope & limitation

Not done: no hierarchical / replicate-level error model; no outlier removal; no Bayesian intervals; no multiple-testing treatment across a plate; no 3-parameter (fixed-slope) or biphasic models; no global fits across plates.

P16README evidence

Antimicrobial peptide classifier

Can antimicrobial activity be predicted from sequence features alone, and how much of the apparent accuracy survives once train and test peptides are properly separated by sequence similarity?

Reported result

Random splitting overstates ROC-AUC by about 0.065 (secreted peptides) and 0.04 (fragments) compared to the purged split.

Scope & limitation

Negative set choice inflates performance and cannot be removed. Both negative sets are assumed inactive (non-antimicrobial annotation in Swiss-Prot), not assayed. They differ from AMPs in origin and processing, so a classifier may partly learn "database/annotation type". Fragments are not real peptides; secreted peptides are few (1,279 after matching) and hard to match on every property. Performance against experimentally confirmed inactive peptides is not measured (not done).

P17README evidence

Differential protein abundance in a public PRIDE benchmark

Can a reproducible differential abundance workflow recover the direction of an E. coli spike-in difference, and how much do missing-value assumptions change the calls?

Reported result

There are 612 missing entries among 30,114 tested sample–protein entries (2.03%). Before the coverage filter, sample missingness ranges from 13.20% to 23.81%; afterward it ranges from 0.46% to 6.63%. These percentages use the corresponding unrounded counts in missingness.tsv.

Scope & limitation

The archive assigns 6/143 fraction files to L1–L3 although their filenames contain Ecoli30: fractions 23 and 24 in each L sample. H1 also has 23 fraction files versus 24 in each other aggregate sample. The processed H/L assignments and published contrast are retained; resolving these source inconsistencies is not done. Per-sample file counts and conflicts are reported in results/samples.tsv.

P24README evidence

Multi-endpoint ADMET — does multitask learning help?

Does a shared molecular representation improve aqueous solubility, Caco-2 permeability and CYP2D6 inhibition predictions compared with independent single-task models?

Reported result

Intervals are two-sided paired t intervals with Bonferroni correction across three endpoints (per-interval confidence 98.3333%, family alpha 0.05). The predeclared practical threshold is 5% relative MAE reduction or 0.02 absolute AP increase, alongside an interval strictly above zero. The evidence supports 0/3 endpoint-level improvement claims. This is insufficient evidence of benefit, rather than proof that the approaches are equivalent.

Scope & limitation

Repeated scaffold splits / split-level uncertainty: not done. Seed intervals describe training randomness conditional on one split. Five differences provide limited support for the t approximation.

P26README evidence

16S microbiome — community differences

Do microbial communities differ among body sites in a public 16S rRNA dataset?

Reported result

PERMANOVA attributes 45.84% of distance variation to body-site grouping in the complete-visit subset. The exact restricted test is not significant at 0.05; the small independent subject count limits inference. Alpha tests also do not pass the threshold after correction. The dispersion diagnostic does not reject, which is not proof of equal dispersion.

Scope & limitation

Only 2 independent participants; repeated visits do not increase independent biological replication. Whole-trajectory exchangeability is an assumption, not a randomized design. Exact p-values have a coarse reference distribution. No population-level or causal conclusion is supported.
