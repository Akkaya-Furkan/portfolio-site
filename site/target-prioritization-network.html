README lines 49–49

Question

In a chosen disease area, which targets stand out on evidence and tractability? Answer for ulcerative colitis (UC): under an equal-weight score, JAK2 and JAK3 rank first; IL23R, TYK2, PPARG, PTGS2, NR3C1 and S1PR1 follow, and the order below the top two is weight-dependent (see sensitivity). This is a database/literature exercise, not a clinical or treatment recommendation.
